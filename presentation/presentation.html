A meta-QTL study identifies stable QTL
for berry quality and yield traits
in cranberry mapping populations (Vaccinium macrocarpon Ait.)

Objectives

  • Map QTL on conventional breeding metrics of crop productivity for yield and quality

  • Generate metaQTL: temporally and semantically stable QTL

  • Connect and validate conventional trait metrics with newer phenotyping methods

  • Develop new marker targets from QTL for optimized breeding

Takeaways

  • genetic signature only found in CNJ02

  • LMI additive genomic heritability is reasonably high (h2=0.74)

  • 11 QTL found, none major

  • Evidence suggests that LMI does not correlate with harvest window

  • Preliminary gene analysis indicates genes tied to circadian rhythms, photoperiod sensing, vernalization, and floral induction
